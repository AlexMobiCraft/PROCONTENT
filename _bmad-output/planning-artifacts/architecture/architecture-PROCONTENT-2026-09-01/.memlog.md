- (decision) Единый DB access-state resolver возвращает has_access, source и valid_until; RLS, middleware RPC и email recipient selector используют его без дублирования business logic; signed cache не живёт дольше valid_until

- (decision) Один grant на offer_code и normalized_purchaser_email; повторная отдельная paid Checkout Session не создаёт второе активное право и не изменяет paid_at/access_ends_at первого grant, а фиксируется как audit exception для отдельно утверждённой refund/support policy

- (decision) Paradigm: DB access-state resolver is the Policy Decision Point; middleware, RLS and email recipient selection are Policy Enforcement Points and may not duplicate access conditions
- (decision) access_entitlements lifecycle: unclaimed to claimed; duplicate_review and ineligible_review never grant; expiry derived from exclusive time predicate and revoked_at, not cron status mutation
- (decision) Claim matches only verified auth.users email normalized by lower(btrim); no provider-specific alias normalization; session_id is UX hint only
- (decision) Resolver returns has_access, sources array, valid_until and evaluated_at so multi-source users do not lose access or email eligibility through source precedence

- (decision) Webhook eligibility uses immutable paid_at rather than receipt time, allowing retry after cutoff for in-window payment and denying new grant for payment after cutoff

- (decision) DB unique offer_code+purchaser_email_normalized with atomic insert-on-conflict selects first committed grant winner; later distinct Sessions are duplicate_review attempts and never replace or extend grant
- (decision) Authoritative purchaser email is retrieved Checkout Session customer_details.email normalized lower(btrim); missing email is non-granting exception
